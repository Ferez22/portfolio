<b>Your posts feel generic? They sound like an AI wrote them? The voicing is off? Your content doesn't go viral?</b>

I spent hours writing and rewriting posts with AI. They were always off, or too AI, or just not my voicing... I found myself always asking for the same changes.

Then I thought to myself, when I code, I give my agent rules and guidelines to follow. Why don't I do that for my posts? After some research about marketing and AI, I found that people have built countless skills and ways to make AI write in their voice. Still they were all too modular. I wanted something so good that all I needed to do was to write one simple prompt and a whole post, including images/videos/motion-graphics were generated, in my voicing, and ready to publish. 

So I built content-studio, a harness around Claude so it interviews me, creates its own understanding of my brands (qartmina, bcards and my personal brand) by crawling the websites and reading the articles I wrote, so I gave it access to Firecrawl.

It was also important to me that it understands the different types of posts I wanted to write (for example educative, informative, promotional, etc) so I gave it a list of examples and rules to follow.

Regarding the media content, I gave it access to Higgsfield and Remotion. Furthermore, I gave it customizable pre-prompts for motion graphics videos, which it deconstructed and saved for future use. 

Then I thought of one thing, I saw on Twitter that someone created a tool to connect Claude to Google Search Console and I thought jackpot, I will add this too, so that content-studio can literally take care of everything online for me, and take actions without me needing to do anything. 

Of course I gave it access to my repos so it can edit my SEO blogs and push them autonomously.

I ended up with a system that generates/edits posts and SEO articles in my voice, cleans up the AI tells, publishes the articles to my website for Google to index, generates media content that matches my style, and everything is ready to publish.

I also decided to open source content-studio, all of it. The best part is you install it with one command, nothing extra is needed.

## Install

<Prompt title="Claude Code">Install this repo, then let's run it: https://github.com/Ferez22/content-studio</Prompt>

Or clone it yourself, run `claude` inside the folder and type `onboarding`. Claude Code loads `CLAUDE.md` on its own; onboarding links the skills, then walks you through your voice and your first brand.

## Use it well

1. **Give it your real best.** The voice guide is only as good as the posts you paste. When it shows you what it noticed about your voice, correct every guess that's off: that's where it learns the most.
2. **Ask for a post, a video or an article.** "Write a post about...", "A launch video for our new feature", "An SEO article about...". Every piece starts as a plan you approve, then you get 3 hooks, a draft and a version per platform.
3. **Correct it in your own words.** Don't just rewrite the draft: say why ("too salesy", "I never open with a question"). Every reason is logged.
4. **Post it yourself, then come back with the results.** "This one got 40 comments" is the best feedback it can get.
5. **Let it learn.** Corrections that repeat become rules, and your results feed the next plan. It proposes changes to your voice guide; nothing changes without your yes.
6. **Feed it.** Drop your hook formulas, sales notes or audience research in `knowledge/`. Every post reads them.

## The checker

Every draft goes through a small script before you see it. It blocks AI tells, em dashes and invisible characters in English, and AI tics, English calques and a mixed tu/vous in French. When you keep deleting the same phrase, it learns that one too.
