Your agent is good. It's just not to your taste.

This fixes that, and it starts with you, not with a template. The first thing it asks for is your own writing: 3 posts you're proud of and 1 you can't stand. It reads them, tells you what it noticed about your voice, and you correct it. From then on, every post starts from that voice and from what you fixed last time.

Under the hood it's a folder of rules, memory and four skills that Claude follows.

It's the free version of my Marketing & SEO Brain: one brand, posts only.

## Use it well

1. **Give it your real best.** The voice guide is only as good as the posts you paste. When it shows you what it noticed about your voice, correct every guess that's off: that's where it learns the most.
2. **Ask for a post.** "Write a post about..." or "What should I post this week?". You get 3 hooks, a draft, and a version per platform.
3. **Correct it in your own words.** Don't just rewrite the draft: say why ("too salesy", "I never open with a question"). Every reason is logged.
4. **Post it yourself, then come back with the results.** "This one got 40 comments" is the best feedback it can get.
5. **Run a weekly review.** Say "weekly review". It turns repeated corrections into rules and proposes changes to your voice guide. Nothing changes without your yes.
6. **Feed it.** Drop your hook formulas, sales notes or audience research in `knowledge/`. Every post reads them.

## The checker

Every draft goes through a small script before you see it. It blocks AI tells, em dashes and invisible characters in English, and AI tics, English calques and a mixed tu/vous in French. When you keep deleting the same phrase, it learns that one too.
