Your agent is good. It's just not to your taste.

You don't need a better model for that. You need a harness around the one you have.

So I built one for marketing and open-sourced it. Clone a folder, type `start`, and about 15 minutes later Claude writes posts in your voice. Every correction you make after that gets logged, and the ones that repeat become rules.

It's the free version of my Marketing & SEO Brain: one brand, posts only, MIT licensed.

## Chatbot, RAG, MCP, harness

Each step keeps the one before it and adds a layer around it.

<HarnessLadder />

Send a message, it's a chatbot. Give it a document, that's RAG. Give it your tools, that's MCP. Build a system around it so it curates itself, learns over time and remembers: that's a harness.

## What it does

- **Learns your voice.** A 15-minute interview turns your best posts into a written voice guide.
- **Writes your posts.** Three hooks, one draft, and a variant per platform (LinkedIn, Instagram, X).
- **Checks every word.** A built-in checker blocks AI tells, em dashes and "translated" French before you see a draft.
- **Remembers corrections.** Every edit you make is logged. The same lesson twice, and it becomes a rule.
- **Never posts for you.** You get copy-ready text. You stay the one who presses publish.

## The loop

This is the part a chatbot doesn't have.

<HarnessLoop />

One correction is an anecdote. Two is a pattern. The harness only changes your voice guide when you say yes.

## The checker

`humanize` ships a small script, standard library Python only. Every draft has to pass it. It comes with an English and a French lexicon: AI tells, em dashes, invisible characters, English calques in French and a mixed tu/vous register.

Here are a few of its rules. Edit the text and check it.

<CheckerDemo />

It grows with you. When you keep deleting the same phrase, `learn` adds it to the lexicon.

## Make it yours

- **Your frameworks:** drop hook formulas, sales tables or audience notes in `knowledge/`. Every post reads them.
- **Your platforms:** list them in `brand/brand.md`. `write-post` makes one variant per platform.
- **Your rules:** edit `CLAUDE.md`. It's plain text, and Claude follows it.
- **Another language:** copy `lexicon/en.json`, turn the patterns into tells of your language, and add `--lang`.
